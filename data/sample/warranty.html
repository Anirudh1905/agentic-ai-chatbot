Warranty

Every Acme-branded product is covered against defects in materials and workmanship. Products from other brands carry their manufacturer's warranty.

Coverage Periods

  • Lifetime warranty: Acme backpacks, tents, sleeping bags and trekking poles.
  • Two years: Acme electronics, including headlamps, GPS units and power banks.
  • One year: Acme apparel and footwear.

What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, damage from improper care, or products altered or repaired by someone other than Acme. Zippers and buckles that wear out after normal use are repaired for a flat $15 fee rather than replaced for free.

How to Make a Claim

Submit a claim at acme-outfitters.example/warranty with a photo of the defect and your order number or receipt. If you don't have a receipt, we can look up purchases made with an Acme Trail Club account. Approved claims are repaired, replaced, or refunded as store credit, at our choice. Most claims are decided within 10 business days.
