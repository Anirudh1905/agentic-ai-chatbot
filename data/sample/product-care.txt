Acme Outfitters Product Care Guide

Tents
Always dry your tent completely before storing it, even if you have to set it up at home. Storing a damp tent for more than 24 hours can cause mildew and break down the waterproof coating. Clean the tent with a sponge and cold water only. Never put a tent in a washing machine or dryer. Store it loosely in a cool, dry place rather than tightly packed in its stuff sack.

Down Jackets and Sleeping Bags
Wash down gear in a front-loading washer on a gentle cycle with a down-specific detergent. Top-loading machines with a center agitator can tear the baffles. Tumble dry on low heat with two or three clean tennis balls to break up clumps. Drying a sleeping bag fully can take three to four hours. Store sleeping bags uncompressed in the large cotton storage sack, not the small compression sack.

Waterproof Jackets
Wash waterproof shells every 20 to 30 days of use, because dirt and body oils clog the membrane. Use a technical wash rather than regular detergent, and skip fabric softener. When water stops beading on the surface, restore the water-repellent finish by tumble drying on medium heat for 20 minutes or applying a spray-on treatment.

Hiking Boots
Remove the insoles and let boots air dry at room temperature after each wet hike. Don't dry them next to a fire or heater, which can crack leather and weaken adhesives. Condition full-grain leather boots two or three times a year.
