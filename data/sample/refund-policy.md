# Refund Policy

Acme Outfitters is a fictional outdoor gear retailer. This policy applies to purchases made in our stores and at acme-outfitters.example.

## Standard Returns

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused, with tags attached, and in the original packaging.

Acme Trail Club members get an extended 60-day return window on all full-price items.

## Sale and Clearance Items

Items bought at 30% off or more are final sale and cannot be returned for a refund. They can be exchanged for a different size of the same item within 14 days, subject to stock.

## Used Gear

Gear that has been used outdoors can be returned within 30 days only if it is defective. Used gear that is not defective is not eligible for a refund. For defects after 30 days, see the Warranty page.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Gift cards
- Opened fuel canisters and stove fuel
- Opened food, including freeze-dried meals and energy bars
- Personalized or engraved items

## Refund Timing

Once we receive and inspect your return, refunds are issued within 5 business days. Your bank may take an additional 3 to 7 business days to show the credit.

Original shipping charges are not refunded, except when the item was defective or we shipped the wrong item.

## Customers in the European Union

EU customers have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal on online orders, in addition to the standard 30-day window. Within those 14 days, we refund the item and the original standard shipping charge, even for sale items.

## How to Start a Return

Start a return from the Orders page of your account or by emailing returns@acme-outfitters.example with your order number. Return shipping labels cost $6.95, which is deducted from your refund. Returns to any store are free.
