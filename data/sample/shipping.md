# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Options (United States)

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|--------|---------------|------|
| Standard | 3 to 7 business days | $7.95, free on orders over $75 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $19.00 |
| Store pickup | Ready in 2 hours at most stores | Free |

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Mountain Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Restricted Items

Fuel canisters, bear spray and lithium batteries over 100 Wh ship by ground only and cannot use Express shipping. They cannot ship to Alaska, Hawaii or PO boxes.

## International Shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International orders take 7 to 14 business days and cost a flat $24.95. Customers pay any import duties and taxes on delivery, except in the EU, where duties are included at checkout.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package shows as delivered but you can't find it, wait 48 hours, then contact support@acme-outfitters.example. We reship lost or damaged orders free of charge once the carrier confirms the claim.

## Order Changes

Orders can be changed or canceled within 30 minutes of being placed. After that, the order is already being packed and must be returned instead.
