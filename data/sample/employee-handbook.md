# Employee Handbook

This handbook covers policies for Acme Outfitters employees in the United States.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued each pay period. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they expire.

Store employees can't take PTO during the two weeks before Thanksgiving through the end of December without manager approval.

## Holidays

Acme observes nine paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, the day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Stores close on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

## Parental Leave

Employees with at least six months of service get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave after the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child. Leave can be taken all at once or in blocks of at least two weeks within 12 months.

## Employee Discount

Employees get 40% off Acme-branded products and 20% off other brands, for themselves and one household member. The discount can't be combined with sale prices or used to buy items for resale.

## Gear Days

Every employee gets two paid Gear Days per year to spend outdoors testing Acme products. Gear Days don't count against PTO and must be scheduled two weeks in advance.

## Remote Work

Corporate office employees can work remotely up to three days a week with manager approval. Store and distribution center roles are on-site.
